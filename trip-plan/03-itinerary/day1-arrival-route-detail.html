Day 1 Arrival Route Detail

Last checked: 2026-05-15.

Target date: 2026-05-30 Sat. Theme: arrival west try day - NRT T3 -> Amber Iidabashi -> Harajuku/Meiji -> optional Tonta -> Amber Iidabashi.

Bottom Line

1일차는 Tonta를 노려볼 수는 있지만, Tonta가 하루의 주인이 되면 안 된다.

Best version:

TimeMove
09:45NRT T3 arrival
10:45-11:15Landside exit, move to Airport Terminal 2/3 Station
11:16 or 11:42Keisei Skyliner from Airport Terminal 2/3
12:40-13:15Amber Iidabashi luggage drop
13:30-14:15Move to AFURI Harajuku
14:15-15:00AFURI Harajuku lunch
15:00-16:00Meiji Jingu light walk
16:00-16:20Decide: Tonta attempt or no
16:20-17:05Move to Tonta
17:05-17:20Line check at Tonta
17:30+Tonta if line is sane, otherwise abandon

Hard rule:

  • If you cannot leave Harajuku/Meiji by 16:20, do not force Tonta.
  • If you cannot stand in the Tonta line by 17:15, treat it as high-risk.
  • If the Tonta line already looks beyond about 25-30 people, skip it. Tonta has 18 seats, so a line that already exceeds the first seating plus much of the second seating is a bad arrival-day bet.

Day 1 Goal

The day should do three things:

  1. Get from NRT T3 to Amber Iidabashi without rushing.
  2. Use Harajuku/Meiji Jingu as a pleasant first Tokyo walk.
  3. Give Tonta one serious try window, while preserving energy if the line is ugly.

It should not do these things:

  • No Golden Gai after Tonta unless energy is weirdly high.
  • No afternoon caffeine. Sedai can be a decaf/non-coffee/beans/rest stop only.
  • No second destination dinner if Tonta fails. Use a nearby easy dinner instead.

Segment Detail

1. NRT T3 Arrival To Airport Terminal 2/3 Station

Expected flow:

  • Flight lands at 09:45.
  • Immigration, baggage, customs, restroom, SIM/Suica/IC adjustments if needed: 45-75 min.
  • Realistic landside exit: 10:30-11:00.
  • Move from T3 arrival lobby to Airport Terminal 2/3 Station:
  • Walk: official estimate is about 13 min from the T3 arrival lobby information counter to the station ticket gates.
  • Shuttle bus: official ride time from T3 to T2 is about 3 min, but wait/walk time can erase the advantage.

Recommendation:

  • Walk if luggage is manageable.
  • Use shuttle if raining, tired, or baggage is annoying.
  • Do not spend decision energy here. Both options are fine.

2. Airport Terminal 2/3 Station To Iidabashi

Recommended route:

  • Keisei Skyliner: Airport Terminal 2/3 -> Nippori.
  • JR: Nippori -> Akihabara -> Iidabashi, or an equivalent one-transfer route.
  • Walk/taxi from Iidabashi Station to Amber Iidabashi.

Useful Skyliner targets from Airport Terminal 2/3:

  • NAVITIME examples show roughly 17-21 min by rail with one transfer.
  • Add 5-10 min for Skyliner-to-JR transfer and platform waiting.
  • Add 10-15 min from Iidabashi Station to Amber Iidabashi.

3. Amber Iidabashi To AFURI Harajuku

Practical route:

  • Amber -> Iidabashi Station: 10-15 min walk.
  • Iidabashi -> Yoyogi or Shinjuku side by JR Chuo-Sobu Local, then Yamanote to Harajuku: 20-30 min train + transfer.
  • Harajuku Station -> AFURI Harajuku: 3-5 min walk.

Estimate:

  • 35-50 min from hotel door to AFURI.

AFURI Harajuku notes:

  • Official AFURI list shows Harajuku open 10:00-23:00, open daily, and closes early if soup runs out.
  • It is cashless.
  • On arrival day, use it as a clean casual lunch, not a long meal.

Target:

  • Sit down/order by 14:15 in the normal plan.
  • Finish by 15:00 if Tonta is still alive.

If AFURI has a long line:

  • If it looks longer than 20-25 min, do not wait.
  • Eat quickly nearby or grab a light snack and preserve Meiji/Tonta.

4. AFURI Harajuku To Meiji Jingu

Meiji Jingu official May hours:

  • Open 05:00-18:10 in May.
  • Some inner facilities have shorter hours, mainly around 09:00-16:00.

Recommended visit style:

  • Do not try to "complete" Meiji Jingu on Day 1.
  • Do a light walk through the Harajuku entrance toward the main shrine area and back.
  • Budget 45-60 min.

Decision gates:

Time after AFURIWhat To Do
Before 15:00Do the light Meiji walk.
15:00-15:25Do a shortened Meiji walk. No Sedai.
After 15:30If Tonta matters, skip Meiji or only touch the entrance.
After 16:00Leave for Tonta or abandon Tonta.

5. Sedai Coffee And Roasters

Role:

  • Optional rest stop near Jingumae/Cat Street.
  • Because of the coffee cutoff rule, this is not a caffeine stop on Day 1 unless you break the rule.
  • Use it only for decaf, non-coffee, beans, water, or a short sit.

Hours note:

  • Listings vary. Several current listings show weekend daytime service, but not all agree on exact hours.
  • Because it is not a core anchor, do not let Sedai decide the day.

Use it only if:

  • You are not trying Tonta, or
  • You finish Meiji early and can still leave the area by 16:20.

6. Harajuku/Meiji/Sedai To Tonta

Target route:

  • Walk back to Harajuku or Meiji-jingumae side.
  • JR Yamanote Line to Takadanobaba.
  • Takadanobaba Station -> Tonta: Tabelog lists 6 min walk.

Estimate:

  • From AFURI/Harajuku Station area: 25-35 min to Tonta.
  • From Sedai/Cat Street area: 35-45 min to Tonta.
  • From inside Meiji Jingu main shrine area: 35-50 min depending on exit.

Leave-by times:

7. Tonta Attempt

Tabelog facts:

  • Reservations unavailable.
  • Open Wed/Fri/Sat 17:30-20:30.
  • Closed Mon/Tue/Thu/Sun/public holidays.
  • 18 seats.
  • Credit cards/e-money/QR not accepted. Bring cash.
  • About 6 min walk from Takadanobaba Station.

For this trip:

  • The only real slot is Sat 2026-05-30 dinner.
  • This is why Day 1 keeps Tonta as a try slot.

Line decision:

Arrival At TontaDecision
16:45-17:05Best try. Join if line feels sane.
17:05-17:15Still possible, but check line carefully.
17:15-17:30Risky. Join only if line is surprisingly short.
After 17:30Usually skip on arrival day.
Line > 25-30 peopleSkip. Too much risk for a tired first day.

Cash:

  • Prepare enough cash before leaving Harajuku.
  • Do not discover the cash issue at the register.

After Tonta:

  • Go back to Amber Iidabashi.
  • Do not add Yamane/Nagi/Open Book. Save Shinjuku for Mon fallback.

TimePlan

AfternoonAFURI Harajuku + short Meiji, or just Iidabashi/Kagurazaka
EveningEasy local dinner near lodging

Verdict: Best choice if the day starts slipping. The rest of the trip is strong enough.

Backup Dinner Ideas If Tonta Is Skipped

Keep it close and easy:

  • Tsujita Iidabashi: easy ramen/tsukemen near lodging.
  • Sake Bar Otonari or Le Ginglet only if hours and mood fit, but do not force a serious night.
  • Convenience store / department-store snack style is acceptable on arrival day if tired.
  • Save Yamane Shoten -> Ramen Nagi -> OPEN BOOK for Mon fallback.
