Day 3 Reservation Dinner Route Detail

Last checked: 2026-05-15

Target date: 2026-06-01 Mon

Theme: reservation dinner first, Shinjuku fallback second. Keep daytime light because Day 2 already has Sake Labo/beer pressure and Day 4 is the room-sake night.

Bottom Line

Day 3 should not be a normal "wander and decide later" day. Monday closes or weakens several lodging-area options, so the clean answer is a decision ladder:

  1. If Sushi Yuuki is booked, make it the only serious dinner and keep the rest of the day soft.
  2. If Sushi Yuuki fails but Torishige is booked or phone-confirmed, make Torishige the dinner anchor.
  3. If neither reservation works, use Yamane Shoten -> Ramen Nagi -> The OPEN BOOK as a Shinjuku mood route.
  4. If tired, stay near Iidabashi/Kagurazaka and make it a recovery night.

This is the day where "best possible dinner" and "fun cheap video route" are different answers. Do not mix them into one overfull night.

Plan A - Sushi Yuuki Reserved

Use this when Sushi Yuuki is booked for Monday dinner.

TimePlan
MorningSleep in. Light breakfast only if hungry.
11:30-12:30Optional light neighborhood walk around Iidabashi/Kagurazaka. Coffee only if before 13:00.
13:00-15:30Rest, shopping, or a very light snack. Do not chase ramen.
Reservation time minus 70 minLeave Amber Iidabashi for Hiroo.
Reservation time minus 10 minArrive near Sushi Yuuki. Do not arrive late.
DinnerSushi Yuuki. This is the main meal.
After dinnerGo back to lodging, or one very light drink near Hiroo/Ebisu only if energy is high. No Nagi after sushi.

Why this wins:

  • It is the highest-upside serious dinner on Day 3.
  • The Tabelog and OMAKASE evidence is strong enough to let it replace the Shinjuku night completely.
  • Sushi Yuuki has fragrance/cancellation rules on OMAKASE, so this needs a clean, respectful day around it.

Rules:

  • No perfume, strong cologne, or strong fabric softener.
  • Confirm OMAKASE course, seat time, cancellation policy, and whether the seat is head-chef or sous-chef before committing.
  • Treat 100% cancellation from 2 days before as a real cost risk.

Plan B - Torishige Reserved

Use this when Sushi Yuuki fails and Torishige is booked or phone-confirmed.

TimePlan
MorningSlow start. No destination breakfast needed.
11:00-12:30Optional coffee before 13:00 if nearby and easy.
13:00-15:30Rest or Shinjuku/Isetan browse. Avoid a heavy lunch.
16:20-16:40Leave Amber if the reservation is around 17:00-17:30.
17:00-19:00Torishige dinner. Bring enough cash.
After dinnerIf still lively, one short Golden Gai drink or Ramen Nagi. If full, return to Amber.

Why this is the best non-sushi answer:

  • Torishige is Tabelog 4.06 and 2026 Bronze, much stronger evidence than the casual video route.
  • It matches the user's "local serious/michelin-level or well-supported dinner" preference better than Yamane.
  • It is close to Shinjuku Station, so the night is logistically easy.

Rules:

  • Do not rely on walk-in. Tabelog explicitly says reservations are available and recommends phone confirmation because temporary weekday closures can occur.
  • Bring cash. Tabelog lists cards, e-money, and QR payments as not accepted.
  • Do not do Yamane before Torishige. Torishige is the dinner, not a second stop.

Plan C - Casual Shinjuku Route

Use this only if both serious reservations fail and the night wants cheap, local, slightly rowdy Shinjuku energy.

TimePlan
16:50-17:10Leave Amber for Higashi-Shinjuku.
17:30-18:40Yamane Shoten. Keep it small: beer, a few skewers, quick local mood.
18:40-19:00Walk toward Golden Gai.
19:00-19:45Ramen Nagi if hungry. Skip if Yamane was enough.
20:00-21:00The OPEN BOOK for lemon sour if there is room.
21:00-22:00Head back before it becomes a late-night crawl.

Why this route works:

  • Yamane closes at 21:00, so it must be first.
  • Ramen Nagi is 24hr, so it absorbs timing issues.
  • OPEN BOOK starts around 20:00, so it fits after Yamane/Nagi.
  • Everything is in the Shinjuku/Higashi-Shinjuku/Golden Gai cluster.

What it is not:

  • Not a replacement for Sushi Yuuki or Torishige as a serious food mission.
  • Not a night to chase every stop. Two of the three is enough if full or tired.

Plan D - Local Recovery Night

Use this if Day 2 was heavy, if reservations failed, or if the body says no.

TimePlan
MorningSleep in.
LunchSimple Iidabashi/Kagurazaka meal. Tsujita is the easy ramen fallback.
AfternoonNap, bath, light walk.
DinnerConvenience store, local casual, or one nearby bar only.

Why:

  • Monday is a bad day for some of the stronger lodging-area candidates: Le Ginglet and Tonkatsu Kenshin are closed.
  • Tuesday already has Kyorakutei + room sake night, so there is no need to force a weak Monday.

RAMEN MATSUI Lunch Option

RAMEN MATSUI is strong enough to know about, but it is not the default Day 3 lunch.

Use it only when:

  • Sushi Yuuki is not booked, or the dinner is late and appetite is clearly good.
  • TableCheck reservation is secured.
  • You can arrive 10 minutes early.
  • You are okay with a 30-minute ramen seating and cash-only ticket-machine payment.

Skip it when:

  • Sushi Yuuki is booked.
  • Torishige is booked early.
  • Day 2 drinking was heavy.
  • You want Day 4 Kyorakutei/room-sake night to stay fresh.
