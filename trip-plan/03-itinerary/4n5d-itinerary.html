4N5D Draft Itinerary

Source: split from ../99-archive/full-notes.md.

Draft Itinerary V1

This is the first realistic 4N5D route based on the shortlisted places.

Latest report-integrated selection: report-picks-route.md. Reservation/wait reality check: reservation-wait-matrix.md. Detailed timing: day1-arrival-route-detail.md, day2-sake-beer-route-detail.md, day3-reservation-dinner-route-detail.md, day4-room-sake-route-detail.md, day5-departure-route-detail.md.

The route below now treats hard queues and reservation-only restaurants differently:

  • Booked places can anchor a day.
  • Long-wait/no-reservation places are only try slots.
  • Walk-in backups stay close to the current area.

Fixed Constraints

  • Flight arrival: 2026-05-30 Sat 09:45 at NRT T3.
  • Flight departure: 2026-06-03 Wed 19:50 from NRT T3.
  • Tonta only works on 2026-05-30 Sat dinner for this trip, but it is not a locked plan because reservations are unavailable and arrival day is fragile.
  • Sake Labo Tokyo is closed Tuesday, and should be treated as reservation-required for this trip.
  • Le Ginglet is open Tue-Sat 18:00-23:00, closed Sunday/Monday.
  • Tonkatsu Kenshin is open Tue/Wed/Fri/Sat/Sun, closed Monday/Thursday.
  • Yamane Shoten closes early around 21:00, so use it as first stop of the night.

Day 1 - 2026-05-30 Sat: Arrival + Optional Tonta Try

Full detail report: day1-arrival-route-detail.md.

Route:

  • 09:45 arrive at Narita T3.
  • Move to Amber Iidabashi, leave luggage or check in if possible.
  • Lunch: AFURI Harajuku.
  • Walk: Meiji Jingu.
  • Break: Sedai Coffee and Roasters only if you are okay with non-coffee/decaf. With the "coffee before 13:00" rule, this is probably not a caffeine stop on arrival day.
  • Early dinner try slot: Tonkatsu Tonta in Takadanobaba only if energy is good. Aim to arrive by 16:45-17:10.
  • After dinner: do not force Yamane Shoten. If Tonta is quick and energy is high, light Shinjuku/Golden Gai is possible, but the better plan is to save Yamane for Monday.

Why:

  • Tonta is the highest Tabelog score and Saturday dinner is the only real chance, but it is reservations-unavailable and only 18 seats.
  • AFURI/Meiji/Sedai makes a clean Harajuku afternoon before moving to Takadanobaba.
  • Keeping Yamane separate avoids rushing a 21:00-close place after a famous tonkatsu queue.

Day 1 Detailed Timing Check

Target principle: protect energy. Tonta is worth trying, but arrival day should not become a punishment march.

Airport to hotel:

  • 09:45 land at NRT T3.
  • Immigration, baggage, and arrival exit buffer: about 45-75 min. Realistic landside time is 10:30-11:00.
  • Move from T3 to Airport Terminal 2・3 Station:
  • Official walking estimate from T3 arrival lobby to the station ticket gates: about 13 min.
  • Free shuttle bus T3 -> T2 ride time: about 3 min, but add waiting/walking time.
  • Recommended rail option: Keisei Skyliner from Airport Terminal 2・3 Station to Nippori, then JR to Iidabashi.
  • Current Keisei weekend examples from Airport Terminal 2・3 Station:
  • 11:16 -> 12:01 Nippori
  • 11:42 -> 12:18 Nippori
  • 12:02 -> 12:38 Nippori
  • Nippori -> Iidabashi with JR transfer buffer: roughly 25-35 min.
  • Iidabashi station -> Amber Iidabashi: roughly 10-15 min walk.
  • Realistic hotel/luggage arrival window: about 12:40-13:20.

Hotel to Harajuku:

  • Amber Iidabashi -> Iidabashi station: about 10-15 min walk.
  • Iidabashi -> Harajuku by JR transfer via Yoyogi/Shinjuku side: about 20-30 min train+transfer.
  • Harajuku station -> AFURI Harajuku: about 3-5 min walk.
  • Realistic hotel -> AFURI total: about 35-50 min.

Harajuku block:

  • AFURI lunch: 30-45 min if line is light; 60 min if there is a line.
  • AFURI -> Meiji Jingu entrance: about 5-10 min walk.
  • Meiji Jingu light visit: 45-60 min.
  • Meiji Jingu -> Sedai Coffee: about 15-25 min depending on where you exit.
  • Optional Sedai break: 20-30 min. With the coffee cutoff, treat this as non-caffeine/skip.
  • Sedai -> Tonta: about 35-45 min including walk to station, JR Yamanote/nearby transfer, and walk from Takadanobaba.

Tonta:

  • Tonta opens at 17:30 and does not take reservations.
  • Aim to be there by 16:45-17:10 if this is the dinner attempt.
  • It is about 6 min walk from Takadanobaba Station.
  • Bring cash. Tabelog lists cards/e-money/QR as not accepted.

Recommended version:

If delayed:

  • If hotel arrival is after 13:30: keep AFURI + Sedai, make Meiji Jingu a short entrance walk only.
  • If hotel arrival is after 14:15: skip Meiji Jingu. Do AFURI -> Sedai -> Tonta.
  • If hotel arrival is after 15:00: skip Harajuku entirely and go directly toward Takadanobaba/Tonta. Use a simple coffee nearby if needed.
  • If too tired to line up for Tonta: abandon Tonta immediately and keep the trip fresh. Use Tonkatsu Kenshin or Kyorakutei/Kenshin-adjacent local options later if tonkatsu still matters.

Day 2 - 2026-05-31 Sun: Coffee + Optional Ramen + Master's Dream + Sake Labo

Full detail and improved route: day2-sake-beer-route-detail.md.

Route:

  • Morning coffee before 13:00: Apollon's Gold Kitanomaru.
  • Optional lunch attempt: Tokyo Style Noodle ほたて日和 in Akihabara only if the entry/registration timing is clean. If the assigned lunch time is after 13:15, skip it.
  • Afternoon: Ginza / Marunouchi.
  • Stop: IMADEYA Ginza for sake browsing or very light tasting only. Do not overdrink before Sake Labo.
  • Beer stop: MASTER'S DREAM HOUSE Marunouchi for Master's Dream. Keep it to 1 hour / 1-2 beers. Reserve if you want a seated stop rather than a quick beer.
  • Dinner: Sake Labo Tokyo, if reservation is available. Best target is 18:00 or 18:30.
  • Back to Iidabashi after dinner.

Why:

  • Apollon's Gold is the best fit from the coffee report for a serious morning coffee near the Iidabashi/Kitanomaru side.
  • Hotate Biyori is the ramen report's strongest one-meal target, but it has too much queue/entry-system risk to be the day's foundation.
  • IMADEYA handles the casual sake-shopping/tasting side.
  • MASTER'S DREAM HOUSE answers the "Suntory Master's Dream properly poured" request without derailing the route.
  • Sake Labo is the dedicated sake dinner of the trip.
  • Sunday works because Le Ginglet is closed, and Sake Labo is open Sunday.
  • The improved plan protects Sake Labo: Hotate Biyori and IMADEYA are both conditional/light, not anchors.

Backup:

  • If Hotate Biyori cannot give a clean return time, skip it and protect Sake Labo. The day is already strong because of Sake Labo + beer.
  • If Sake Labo is not available Sunday, try Monday 2026-06-01.
  • If Sake Labo moves to Monday, shift the Shinjuku route to Tuesday and drop Le Ginglet.

Day 3 - 2026-06-01 Mon: Reservation Dinner Night

Full detail and movement gates: day3-reservation-dinner-route-detail.md.

Plan A - if Sushi Yuuki reservation succeeds:

  • Daytime: keep flexible and light.
  • Dinner: Sushi Yuuki as the main meal.
  • After dinner: one light drink only if energy is high. Do not add Ramen Nagi as a second full meal.

Why:

  • Sushi Yuuki is the sushi report's best-fit target: serious enough, not only trophy dining, and still realistic compared with the hardest Tokyo counters.
  • A real sushi dinner should be the anchor of the day, not an add-on after ramen or yakitori.

Plan B - if Sushi Yuuki fails:

  • Daytime: keep flexible. Sleep in, shop, or do a light neighborhood day.
  • Dinner upgrade: Torishige Shinjuku only if reserved or phone-confirmed.
  • Casual fallback: Yamane Shoten around opening time -> Ramen Nagi Shinjuku Golden Gai -> The OPEN BOOK.
  • Easy fallback: stay near Iidabashi/Kagurazaka and keep the night simple.

Why:

  • Monday is bad for Le Ginglet/Kenshin because both are closed.
  • Yamane is open Monday and works best as an early 0차.
  • Nagi and OPEN BOOK are close enough to make this a clean video-route night.
  • Torishige is the better choice when "진짜 갈만한 집" matters more than cheap/casual Shinjuku mood, but do not depend on walk-in.

Optional lunch:

  • RAMEN MATSUI is a strong Shinjuku lunch candidate only if TableCheck reservation is secured and the dinner is not Sushi Yuuki/Torishige, or appetite is excellent.
  • Default is to skip it when a serious dinner is booked. Monday should stay fresh, not stacked.

Tabelog upgrade:

  • If this night should become a real food mission, replace Yamane Shoten with Torishige Shinjuku.
  • Torishige is a much more serious motsuyaki option, but it is not the cheap/video-route version of the night.
  • If Torishige is not reserved, Yamane/Nagi/OPEN BOOK is a mood route, not a food-proof route.

Day 4 - 2026-06-02 Tue: Room Sake Night

Full detail and shopping gates: day4-room-sake-route-detail.md.

Main plan:

  • Slow local day around Iidabashi/Kagurazaka.
  • Lunch: Kyorakutei for soba/tempura.
  • Lunch backup: Sanntora for serious local ramen if Kyorakutei is not the mood or Hotate Biyori failed earlier.
  • Late afternoon: go to Tokyo Station.
  • Buy food: Daimaru Tokyo Hoppe Town B1, Gransta Tokyo, or Kinokuniya Entree Gransta.
  • Buy sake last: Hasegawa Saketen Gransta Tokyo.
  • Return to Amber Iidabashi.
  • Dinner: room sake night with one serious 720ml sake and depachika/ekinaka food.

Shopping target:

  • Sake: ask Hasegawa staff for a chilled 720ml bottle with umami and acidity, ideally seasonal nama or a staff pick that works with sushi/sashimi and deli sides.
  • Food: sushi or sashimi, one fried/grilled side, pickles or vegetables, and maybe a small sweet.

Why:

  • This adds one uniquely Japanese night without another reservation or queue.
  • Tokyo Station is close enough to Iidabashi that chilled sake and fresh food can be carried back safely.
  • It balances the trip after destination meals like Tonta and Sake Labo.
  • The most important execution detail is to buy chilled sake last and return directly to the room.

Reference:

  • Room sake plan: ../04-food-drink/sake/room-sake-night.md
  • Report-integrated picks: report-picks-route.md
  • Reservation/wait matrix: reservation-wait-matrix.md

Backup if Tonta failed on Saturday:

  • Use Tonkatsu Kenshin as lunch or early dinner on Tuesday, then keep the room sake plan lighter.
  • If Kenshin becomes the full dinner, move room sake night to Monday after a shorter Shinjuku route or make it a late snack-only plan.

Backup if too tired to go to Tokyo Station:

  • Use Iidabashi/Kagurazaka supermarket or nearby deli, but this is lower priority than Hasegawa + Daimaru/Gransta.

High-end Tabelog upgrade:

  • If a strong reservation opens, Kasahara, Kagurazaka Ishikawa, Kohaku, or L'ETERRE can take over Tuesday dinner.
  • In that case, move room sake night to a lighter late snack or drop it.

Day 5 - 2026-06-03 Wed: Light Iidabashi + Airport

Full detail and airport gates: day5-departure-route-detail.md.

Route:

  • Solve luggage early. Confirm Amber Iidabashi storage, or use station lockers and keep the day near Iidabashi.
  • Late breakfast / early lunch near lodging.
  • Default lunch: Tsujita Iidabashi.
  • Catch-up lunch only if everything is smooth: Kyorakutei if missed Tuesday, Tonkatsu Kenshin if Tonta failed, Sanntora if ramen mood is strong.
  • Pack and keep the afternoon light.
  • Aim to leave Amber/luggage point around 15:30-15:45 for Narita.
  • Target Nippori 16:25 or 16:45 Skyliner. Treat 17:05 as recovery, not the plan.
  • 19:50 depart from NRT T3.

Why:

  • Departure day should not carry a destination meal.
  • Tsujita is close, easy, and good enough without risking airport timing.
  • Narita official guidance recommends arriving at least two hours before international departure, and the T2/3 station to T3 walking corridor adds about 13 minutes before check-in-area movement.

Reservation / Decision List

  1. Try to reserve Sake Labo Tokyo for Sunday 2026-05-31 dinner.
  2. If Sunday Sake Labo fails, try Monday 2026-06-01 dinner.
  3. Try Sushi Yuuki for Monday 2026-06-01 dinner or any clean dinner slot. If it succeeds, let it replace Shinjuku dinner rather than adding more food after it.
  4. If Sushi Yuuki fails, try Torishige for Monday dinner. If it is not reserved or phone-confirmed, do not count on it.
  5. Keep Sunday morning coffee to Apollon's Gold and do not add afternoon caffeine.
  6. Attempt Hotate Biyori only if the entry/registration timing is clean. Skip it if it threatens Sake Labo.
  7. Treat Tonta as an arrival-day try slot, not a must. If the line or fatigue is bad, skip it.
  8. Keep Tuesday 2026-06-02 for Kyorakutei lunch and room sake night unless a truly stronger dinner reservation wins.
