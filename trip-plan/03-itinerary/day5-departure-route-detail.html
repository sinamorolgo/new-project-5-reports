Day 5 Departure Route Detail

Last checked: 2026-05-15

Target date: 2026-06-03 Wed

Flight: Jeju Air 7C1110, NRT Terminal 3 -> ICN Terminal 1, 19:50-22:45.

Theme: light Iidabashi, luggage solved early, no destination meal, arrive NRT T3 with enough margin.

Bottom Line

Day 5 is not a food mission day. The best version is:

Amber Iidabashi
-> solve luggage
-> light Iidabashi lunch
-> pick up luggage / final repack
-> Iidabashi Station
-> Nippori
-> Keisei Skyliner
-> Airport Terminal 2/3 Station
-> walk or shuttle to NRT Terminal 3

Use Nippori 16:25 or 16:45 Skyliner as the real target. For a 19:50 international departure, the 17:05 Skyliner is a recovery train, not the plan.

Luggage Plan

Best Case

  • Amber lets you leave luggage after checkout.
  • Eat near Iidabashi.
  • Return by 14:30-15:00.
  • Leave for Nippori at 15:30-15:45.

If Amber Cannot Hold Luggage

Use one of these:

  1. Iidabashi Station coin lockers if available.
  2. Keep luggage with you and only eat at Tsujita or a very close place.
  3. Taxi from Amber to Nippori if carrying bags through transfers feels bad.

Do not use the final day to roam with luggage. It makes every good plan brittle.

Lunch Plan

Plan A - Tsujita Iidabashi

Use this by default.

Why:

  • Very close to Iidabashi Station.
  • Reservations unavailable but operationally simple.
  • Works whether luggage storage succeeds or fails.
  • Cheap, fast, and good enough without becoming a destination meal.

Order direction:

  • Regular tsukemen or ramen.
  • Avoid over-ordering. Airport day is better with a light stomach.

Map:

  • Amber Iidabashi -> Tsujita Iidabashi: https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Amber+Iidabashi+Shinjuku-ku+Shinogawamachi+5-13&destination=Tsujita+Iidabashi&travelmode=walking

Plan B - Kyorakutei Catch-Up

Use this only if:

  • You missed Kyorakutei on Tuesday.
  • Luggage is solved.
  • You can arrive before 11:20.
  • The line looks light.

Skip it if there is any luggage ambiguity. A soba queue on airport day is a tax on your future self.

Map:

  • Amber Iidabashi -> Kyorakutei: https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Amber+Iidabashi+Shinjuku-ku+Shinogawamachi+5-13&destination=Kyorakutei+Kagurazaka&travelmode=walking

Plan C - Tonkatsu Kenshin Catch-Up

Use this only if:

  • Tonta failed and tonkatsu still matters.
  • Luggage is stored.
  • You are okay with a slower, heavier lunch.
  • You can sit down early and leave the restaurant by 13:00-13:30.

This is a good restaurant, but it is not the best departure-day shape.

Map:

  • Amber Iidabashi -> Tonkatsu Kenshin: https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Amber+Iidabashi+Shinjuku-ku+Shinogawamachi+5-13&destination=Tonkatsu+Kenshin+Kagurazaka&travelmode=walking

Plan D - Sanntora

Use this only if:

  • You specifically want miso ramen.
  • There is no visible line/sellout issue.
  • It is still early.

Sanntora is stronger than Tsujita as a ramen pick, but Tsujita is stronger as a flight-day move.

Route Steps

  1. Amber / luggage point -> Iidabashi Station.
  2. Iidabashi -> Nippori.
  3. Transfer to Keisei Skyliner at Nippori.
  4. Skyliner to Airport Terminal 2/3 Station.
  5. Walk the T2/3 to T3 corridor unless luggage makes shuttle clearly easier.
  6. Go to Terminal 3 2F check-in counter area.
