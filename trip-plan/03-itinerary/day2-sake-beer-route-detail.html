Day 2 Sake And Beer Route Detail

Last checked: 2026-05-15.

Target date: 2026-05-31 Sun. Theme: coffee + conditional ramen + Suntory beer + Sake Labo anchor.

Bottom Line

2일차의 주인공은 Sake Labo Tokyo다. Hotate Biyori는 맛으로는 강하지만, 일요일 오전 기명/정리권 리스크가 커서 하루의 중심에 두면 안 된다.

Improved default:

TimeMove
09:05-09:15Leave Amber Iidabashi
09:30-10:20Apollon's Gold Kitanomaru
10:20-10:50Move toward Akihabara only if Hotate Biyori check is worth it
10:50-11:10Hotate Biyori entry/diary check
11:10-13:00Eat Hotate only if assigned lunch entry is clean; otherwise skip
13:00-14:00Move to Ginza/Marunouchi, light lunch if Hotate failed
14:00-14:30IMADEYA GINZA browse/light tasting only
15:30-16:30MASTER'S DREAM HOUSE Marunouchi
16:40-17:30Move to Jujo / buffer
18:00 or 18:30Sake Labo Tokyo reservation target
After dinnerReturn to Amber Iidabashi. No second stop.

Main improvement from the previous route:

  • Hotate Biyori is now a conditional check, not a lunch anchor.
  • Sake Labo should be booked around 18:00-18:30 if possible.
  • MASTER'S DREAM HOUSE should be a controlled 1-hour beer stop, not a full meal.
  • IMADEYA should be browse/light tasting only because Sake Labo is already the sake dinner.

Route Logic

The strongest route shape is:

Iidabashi
-> Kitanomaru coffee
-> optional Akihabara ramen check
-> Ginza / Marunouchi
-> Tokyo Station beer
-> Jujo Sake Labo
-> Iidabashi

This looks long on paper, but each segment is reasonable. The danger is not distance. The danger is waiting too long for Hotate, then arriving at Sake Labo already tired, full, and lightly drunk.

Segment Detail

1. Amber Iidabashi To Apollon's Gold Kitanomaru

Goal:

  • Start with good coffee, not a rush.
  • Keep caffeine before 13:00.

Timing:

  • Leave Amber around 09:05-09:15.
  • Arrive around opening, 09:30.
  • Stay 35-50 min.

Notes:

  • Apollon's Gold Kitanomaru opens at 09:30 on weekends.
  • It has 10+ single-origin espresso options and signature pourover setup, so this is a real coffee stop, not just "카페 들름".
  • If the previous night after Tonta was heavy, this coffee block can become the recovery block.

2. Apollon's Gold To Hotate Biyori Check

Default:

  • Leave Apollon's by 10:20 if trying Hotate.
  • Reach Akihabara / Hotate area around 10:45-10:55.

Direct rail reference:

  • Iidabashi -> Akihabara is about 7 min by JR Chuo-Sobu Local, but door-to-door with walking and station movement is closer to 20 min.
  • Kitanomaru/Kudanshita -> Akihabara is roughly 20-30 min door-to-door depending on route and walk.

Hotate facts:

  • Tabelog lists reservations unavailable.
  • Tabelog also notes an entry/diary system, with timing based on Twitter/X.
  • A visit report describes signing a waiting sheet from 10:00 and receiving an assigned return time.
  • Current reviews imply the line can start much earlier on busy days.

Decision:

Situation At HotateDecision
Assigned entry by 12:30Eat. This is clean.
Assigned entry 12:30-13:15Eat only if Sake Labo is 18:30 or later and energy is good.
Assigned entry after 13:15Skip. It starts damaging the day.
Only evening slot around 16:50Skip. It collides with beer and Sake Labo appetite.
Sold out / confusing diary system / long crowdSkip immediately.

Important:

  • Do not wait physically for hours.
  • Do not use Hotate dinner on this day. 16:50 ramen before Sake Labo is the wrong kind of heroic.
  • Bring cash if you do eat; Tabelog lists cards/e-money/QR not accepted.

3. If Hotate Works

Good-case flow:

TimePlan
10:50-11:10Register/check entry
11:30-12:45Eat Hotate Biyori if assigned
12:45-13:20Move toward Ginza/Marunouchi
13:20-14:00Light walk / reset, no extra heavy food
14:00-14:30IMADEYA browse or very small tasting
15:30-16:30MASTER'S DREAM HOUSE
18:00/18:30Sake Labo

Why this works:

  • You get the ramen mission without sacrificing the evening.
  • You still have a buffer before beer and Sake Labo.

What to avoid:

  • Do not add Ginza Hachigo or another ramen.
  • Do not turn IMADEYA into a full tasting session.
  • Do not order a heavy food course at MASTER'S DREAM HOUSE.

4. If Hotate Fails

This is not a failed day. This is probably the cleaner version.

Recommended flow:

TimePlan
10:50-11:10Hotate check fails
11:10-12:00Move to Ginza / Marunouchi
12:00-13:15Light lunch or Marunouchi/Ginza walk
14:00-14:30IMADEYA GINZA browse/light tasting
15:30-16:30MASTER'S DREAM HOUSE
18:00/18:30Sake Labo

Lunch principle:

  • Keep it light. Sake Labo is dinner.
  • Do not replace Hotate with another destination queue.
  • If you want ramen backup, save it for Tue catch-up instead.

Good use of the extra time:

  • Ginza / Marunouchi walk.
  • IMADEYA bottle browsing for future room sake ideas.
  • Rest at a quiet cafe without caffeine.
  • Arrive near Jujo early and walk the shopping street lightly before Sake Labo.

5. IMADEYA GINZA

Role:

  • Sake browsing and maybe a tiny tasting.
  • Good place to learn styles before Sake Labo, but not the main sake experience.

Facts:

  • Kakuuchi hours: 14:00-20:00, last order 19:30.
  • Reservation not needed and not accepted.
  • English support available.
  • Standing only.
  • 20+ sake/wine/etc. options, including 2-kind or 3-kind comparison.

Use on Day 2:

  • Best as a 20-30 min stop.
  • If doing Sake Labo dinner, keep tasting to 2-3 small pours max, or just browse.
  • Do not buy room-night sake here unless you want to carry it all day. Hasegawa + Tokyo Station is better for Tue room sake night.

6. MASTER'S DREAM HOUSE Marunouchi

Role:

  • Suntory Master's Dream / proper pour stop.
  • This answers the "푸어링까지 잘하는 맥주집" request without derailing the day.

Facts:

  • Sunday hours: 12:00-22:00.
  • Located about 1 min from JR Tokyo Station.
  • Master's Dream is on the official menu.
  • The TASTING4 set includes Master's Dream, Premium Malt's, Fragrant Ale, and Black.

Best use:

  • Reserve or plan for 15:30-16:30 if Sake Labo is at 18:00.
  • Order Master's Dream or TASTING4.
  • Keep food light: fish and chips/share snack if needed, not a full course.

Kill switch:

  • If Sake Labo is at 18:00 and MASTER'S DREAM HOUSE is crowded by 16:00, skip beer or make it one quick glass.
  • Do not arrive at Sake Labo full.

7. MASTER'S DREAM HOUSE To Sake Labo Tokyo

Target:

  • Leave Marunouchi/Tokyo Station around 16:40-17:00 for an 18:00 reservation.
  • Leave around 17:10-17:30 for an 18:30 reservation.

Estimated route:

  • Tokyo / Marunouchi -> Akabane or Ikebukuro side -> Jujo.
  • Door-to-door with station walking: 35-45 min.
  • Sake Labo is about 3 min walk from Jujo Station.

Why the buffer matters:

  • Tokyo Station is easy to underestimate.
  • Sake Labo has cancellation policies and is the anchor.
  • Arriving early in Jujo is better than sprinting through Tokyo Station after beer.

8. Sake Labo Tokyo

Role:

  • Main dinner.
  • This is the day's highest-priority booking.

Facts:

  • Sunday/public holiday hours: 15:00-22:30, food L.O. 21:30, drink L.O. 22:00.
  • Reservations available.
  • Busy forecast dates may be course-only.
  • Cancellation fees apply.
  • Around 28 seats.
  • Credit card, transit IC, and QR payments accepted.

Best reservation time:

TimeVerdict
17:30Very good if you want an early night, but compresses MDH.
18:00Best default.
18:30Also good; gives beer stop more room.
19:00+Possible, but makes the night longer after a busy day.

After dinner:

  • Return to Amber Iidabashi.
  • No Nagi, no Golden Gai, no extra ramen.
  • If you want one more drink, do it at the room.

Verdict: Without Sake Labo, Hotate can become more important. But do not build this version unless the reservation fails.

Hard Rules

  • If Hotate gives a return time after 13:15, skip.
  • If Hotate gives only an evening time, skip.
  • If Sake Labo is booked at 18:00, leave Tokyo Station/Marunouchi by 16:45-17:00.
  • If doing IMADEYA and MASTER'S DREAM HOUSE before Sake Labo, keep both small.
  • If Day 1 Tonta happened and you are heavy/tired, use Version B.
