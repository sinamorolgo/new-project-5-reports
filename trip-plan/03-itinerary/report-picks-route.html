Report Picks Route

리포트 후보를 다시 줄인 현실형 일정이다. 이번 기준은 "진짜 가볼 만한가"와 "이번 4박 5일에 실패 확률이 낮은가"를 분리하는 것이다.

Core Rule

  • 기본 일정은 예약 실패나 긴 줄 때문에 무너지면 안 된다.
  • 예약 전용 식당은 예약 성공 시에만 일정에 넣는다.
  • 예약불가+긴 대기 식당은 "시도 슬롯"으로만 둔다.
  • 커피는 13:00 이전에만 넣는다.
  • 저녁은 술 또는 미식 중심으로 잡고, 밤에 과식 루트를 강제로 붙이지 않는다.

Optional swaps and route variants: optional-route-playbook.md.

Risk-Adjusted 4N5D Route

Day 1 - 2026-05-30 Sat

Theme: arrival day, do not let Tonta break the trip.

Default:

  1. NRT T3 -> Amber Iidabashi.
  2. AFURI Harajuku for easy casual lunch if timing works.
  3. Meiji Jingu light walk only if arrival is smooth.
  4. Early evening decision:
  • Good energy and arrival not delayed: try Tonta line-up by 16:45-17:10.
  • Tired or delayed: skip Tonta and eat easy near Iidabashi/Kagurazaka.

Why:

  • Tonta is genuinely worth trying, but it is reservation-free, tiny, and only available on Sat for this trip.
  • Treating it as "must" on arrival day creates too much pressure.

Day 2 - 2026-05-31 Sun

Theme: low-risk coffee + optional ramen attempt + booked sake night.

Detailed route and decision gates: day2-sake-beer-route-detail.md.

Default:

  1. 09:30-10:30: Apollon's Gold Kitanomaru.
  2. 10:45: decide Hotate Biyori.
  • If registration/entry timing is clean and assigned lunch is not after 13:15: eat Hotate Biyori.
  • If timing is ugly: skip it, do IMADEYA Ginza or a light Marunouchi lunch.
  1. Afternoon: IMADEYA Ginza if there is time, but keep it light.
  2. Beer: MASTER'S DREAM HOUSE Marunouchi. Reserve if planning to sit and drink properly. Keep it to 1 hour / 1-2 beers.
  3. Dinner: Sake Labo Tokyo only if reserved. Best target is 18:00 or 18:30.

Why:

  • Sake Labo is the real anchor. Hotate Biyori is a high-upside attempt, not the day's foundation.
  • MASTER'S DREAM HOUSE is the most reliable way to satisfy the Suntory pouring request without a detour.
  • IMADEYA and MASTER'S DREAM HOUSE should support Sake Labo, not exhaust the palate before it.

Day 3 - 2026-06-01 Mon

Theme: reservation dinner night.

Detailed route and movement gates: day3-reservation-dinner-route-detail.md.

Plan A:

  1. Sushi Yuuki, if reservation succeeds.
  2. Keep the rest of the day light. No second full meal after sushi.

Plan B:

  1. Torishige Shinjuku, if reservation succeeds.
  2. Bring enough cash.

Plan C:

  1. If neither reservation works, use the casual Shinjuku route only as a mood route: Yamane Shoten -> Ramen Nagi -> OPEN BOOK.
  2. If tired, stay near Iidabashi/Kagurazaka and do a simple local dinner.

Why:

  • Monday should not depend on a walk-in miracle.
  • Sushi Yuuki and Torishige are both worth building a night around, but only with a reservation.
  • RAMEN MATSUI is a real lunch upgrade only if reserved, but skip it by default when Sushi Yuuki or Torishige is booked.

Day 4 - 2026-06-02 Tue

Theme: local lunch + best low-friction night.

Detailed route and shopping gates: day4-room-sake-route-detail.md.

Default:

  1. Lunch: Kyorakutei Kagurazaka.
  2. If Kyorakutei is too busy or closed unexpectedly: Sanntora, then Tsujita as the easy fallback.
  3. Late afternoon: Tokyo Station.
  4. Food: Daimaru Tokyo Hoppe Town or Gransta.
  5. Buy chilled sake last at Hasegawa Saketen Gransta Tokyo.
  6. Dinner: room sake night at Amber Iidabashi.

Why:

  • This is the most robust day in the plan: good lunch, low-risk shopping, and no dinner reservation pressure.
  • It also gives the trip one night that feels very Japanese without another queue.
  • The execution rule is food first, chilled sake last, then straight back to Iidabashi.

Day 5 - 2026-06-03 Wed

Theme: departure-safe day.

Detailed airport route and kill switches: day5-departure-route-detail.md.

  1. Solve luggage early.
  2. No destination meal.
  3. Tsujita or simple Iidabashi lunch by default.
  4. Leave Amber/luggage point around 15:30-15:45.
  5. Target Nippori 16:25 or 16:45 Skyliner for NRT T3.

Booking Order

  1. Sake Labo Tokyo for 2026-05-31 Sun dinner.
  2. Sushi Yuuki for 2026-06-01 Mon dinner.
  3. Torishige for 2026-06-01 Mon dinner if Sushi Yuuki fails.
  4. MASTER'S DREAM HOUSE Marunouchi if you want seated beer rather than a quick stop.

Practical Final Shape

  • Sat: arrival + optional Tonta attempt.
  • Sun: Apollon's Gold -> optional Hotate Biyori -> MASTER'S DREAM HOUSE -> Sake Labo.
  • Mon: Sushi Yuuki if booked; otherwise Torishige if booked; otherwise casual/local.
  • Tue: Kyorakutei -> Daimaru/Gransta food -> Hasegawa chilled sake -> room sake.
  • Wed: luggage solved -> light Iidabashi lunch -> Nippori 16:25/16:45 Skyliner -> NRT T3.

This version keeps the truly good places, but stops pretending every famous queue is a reliable itinerary item.

If the mood shifts, use optional-route-playbook.md to decide what gets swapped out. The important thing is that options should replace a block, not sit on top of it.
