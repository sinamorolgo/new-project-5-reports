Day 4 Room Sake Route Detail

Last checked: 2026-05-15

Target date: 2026-06-02 Tue

Theme: Kagurazaka local lunch, Tokyo Station sake/depachika shopping, then a serious room-sake dinner at Amber Iidabashi.

Bottom Line

Day 4 should stay low-friction. The best version is not another reservation dinner; it is a good lunch near the lodging, then buying a serious chilled sake bottle and high-quality food hall snacks before returning quickly to the room.

Best execution:

Amber Iidabashi
-> Kyorakutei lunch
-> rest / light Kagurazaka walk
-> Tokyo Station
-> Daimaru Hoppe Town or Gransta food
-> Hasegawa Saketen Gransta Tokyo sake
-> Amber Iidabashi room sake dinner

The key trick: buy chilled sake last, then go straight back.

Lunch Plan

Plan A - Kyorakutei

Use this by default.

Order direction:

  • Soba + tempura set if hungry.
  • Seiro/zaru style if you want to keep dinner appetite alive.
  • Add one small side only if the line and pacing are relaxed.

Why:

  • It gives Day 4 a real food highlight without a dinner reservation.
  • It is close enough that a line does not ruin the day.
  • Soba/tempura is satisfying but lighter than ramen or tonkatsu before room sake.

Kill switch:

  • If the line looks like 45+ minutes at 11:20, do not turn the recovery day into a queue day. Go Sanntora if ramen mood is strong, or Tsujita if you want easy mode.

Plan B - Sanntora

Use this if:

  • Kyorakutei is unexpectedly closed or the line is bad.
  • You still want a real local high-score meal.
  • It is still early enough that sellout risk is not annoying.

Rules:

  • Check day-of SNS/Google before going if possible.
  • Bring cash.
  • If it is already late lunch, skip to Tsujita.

Map:

  • Amber Iidabashi -> Sanntora: https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Amber+Iidabashi+Shinjuku-ku+Shinogawamachi+5-13&destination=Sanntora+Edogawabashi&travelmode=transit

Plan C - Tsujita Iidabashi

Use this if:

  • You are tired.
  • Weather is bad.
  • Kyorakutei and Sanntora both look like work.

This is not the best food mission, but it is the best recovery answer.

Shopping Plan

Best Order

  1. Go to Tokyo Station around 16:00.
  2. If you want staff advice, preview Hasegawa first and ask what they recommend.
  3. Buy food at Daimaru Hoppe Town or Gransta.
  4. Buy chilled sake at Hasegawa last.
  5. Return directly to Amber.

This avoids carrying nama/chilled sake around Tokyo Station too long.

Sake Ask

Use this at Hasegawa:

ホテルで惣菜と飲みたいです。2人で720mlくらい。
香りだけじゃなく、旨味と酸があって、東京旅行で飲む価値のある日本酒をおすすめしてください。
生酒や季節限定があれば見たいです。
保冷バッグはありますか？

Meaning:

  • We want to drink it at the hotel with prepared foods.
  • Around 720ml for two people.
  • Not just aromatic; umami and acidity are important.
  • Show us seasonal or nama sake if available.
  • Do you have a cold bag?

Bottle Strategy

StrategyBuyWhen
Simple and bestOne serious chilled 720mlDefault. Two people, one night, no overthinking.
GeekierTwo 300ml/500ml bottlesIf the staff has two distinct styles and small sizes.
Low-energyOne staff pick + sparkling waterIf Day 3 was heavy.

Good target styles:

  • Clean food-friendly: Hakurakusei, Isojiman, Zaku, Kuheiji, Sharaku.
  • Modern acidity or nama: Kaze no Mori, Senkin, AKABU, Mutsu Hassen.
  • If rare names like Juyondai, Aramasa, or Jikon appear at sane terms, consider them, but do not plan the night around unicorn bottles.

Food Basket

Main Basket

Buy:

  • sushi/sashimi or anago sushi
  • one fried or grilled side
  • pickles or vegetable side
  • sparkling water
  • small wagashi or fruit

Why:

  • This covers clean fish pairing, richer sake pairing, palate reset, and after-drink sweetness.

Pairing Logic

Daimaru Targets

The Hoppe Town guide has the exact type of counters that work for this night:

  • B1 bento/sushi such as anago sushi, creative sushi, crab/salmon-style sushi counters.
  • B1 prepared foods such as roast beef, grilled fish, shrimp mayo, salads, Japanese deli.
  • 1F sweets if you want a proper finish.

Gransta / Kinokuniya Backup

Use Gransta if Daimaru is too crowded or if you want fewer station exits.

Use Kinokuniya Entree if:

  • You need cups, water, snacks, bread, or an easy backup.
  • You missed Daimaru's 20:00 closing window.
  • You want a smaller basket instead of full depachika browsing.

Room Setup

Before opening the bottle:

  • Put chilled food and sake in the fridge for 10-15 minutes if the room has space.
  • Buy two small cups if the room glassware is bad.
  • Open sparkling water first.
  • Eat raw fish early, fried/grilled food later.
  • Stop around 21:00-21:30. The next day is airport day.
