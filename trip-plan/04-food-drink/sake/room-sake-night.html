Room Sake Night

Last updated: 2026-05-15 KST

Goal: 하루 정도는 외식 예약을 비우고, 진짜 좋은 사케와 데파치카/역 식품관 안주를 사 와서 숙소에서 느긋하게 먹는다.

Detailed Day 4 route: ../../03-itinerary/day4-room-sake-route-detail.md.

Best Day

Use this on Day 4 - 2026-06-02 Tue.

Why:

  • 여행 후반이라 밖에서 또 줄 서고 이동하는 피로를 줄일 수 있음.
  • 다음날 2026-06-03 Wed는 귀국일이라 너무 늦게 마시지 않는 게 좋음.
  • Tokyo Station -> Iidabashi 이동이 짧아 신선한 안주와 냉장 사케를 들고 오기 좋음.
  • Sake Labo Tokyo와는 다른 결: 코스/페어링이 아니라 직접 고르는 사케덕후형 밤.

Where To Buy Sake

Main Pick: Hasegawa Saketen Gransta Tokyo

  • Search: Hasegawa Saketen Gransta Tokyo
  • Address: B1, 1-9-1 Marunouchi, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo
  • Hours checked on official Hasegawa page: 08:00-21:00 daily.
  • Why: 도쿄역 직결, 좋은 일본주 큐레이션, 여행자가 사케와 안주를 한 번에 챙기기 쉬운 위치.
  • Use: 직원에게 냉장 사케, 계절 한정, 生酒, 720ml 기준으로 추천받기.

Ask like this:

ホテルで惣菜と飲みたいです。2人で720mlくらい。
香りだけじゃなく、旨味と酸があって、東京旅行で飲む価値のある日本酒をおすすめしてください。
生酒や季節限定があれば見たいです。

Korean meaning: 숙소에서 안주랑 마실 건데 2명이 720ml 정도, 향만 화려한 것보다 감칠맛과 산이 있고 여행에서 마실 가치 있는 사케를 추천해달라는 뜻.

Backup: IMADEYA GINZA

  • Already in the trip list.
  • Ginza day에 이미 들른다면 여기서 사케를 사서 숙소로 가져가도 좋음.
  • Use when the day already includes Ginza Six / IMADEYA.

What Sake To Buy

Do not plan around impossible bottles. 十四代, 新政, 而今 같은 병은 있으면 좋지만, 재고/추첨/가격 변수가 크다. 직원이 권하면 고려하고, 없으면 아래 스타일을 노리는 게 안전하다.

One-Bottle Plan

For 2 people, buy one 720ml bottle.

Best target:

  • 寫樂 / Sharaku: 회, 초밥, 조림까지 폭넓게 좋음.
  • 醸し人九平次 / Kuheiji: 산미와 구조감이 있어 데파치카 안주와 잘 맞음.
  • 磯自慢 / Isojiman: 회/초밥 쪽으로 깨끗하고 고급스러운 방향.
  • 作 / Zaku: 향과 밸런스가 좋아 실패 확률 낮음.
  • 伯楽星 / Hakurakusei: 음식과 맞추기 좋은 식중주 타입.
  • 風の森 / Kaze no Mori: 산, 가스감, 생동감. 튀김/치즈/짭짤한 안주와 재미있음.
  • 赤武 / AKABU, 陸奥八仙 / Mutsu Hassen, 仙禽 / Senkin: 현대적인 산미/향/밸런스가 좋아 여행 사케로 재미있음.

What Food To Buy

Main Food Hall: Daimaru Tokyo Hoppe Town

  • Search: Daimaru Tokyo Hoppe Town
  • Address: 1-9-1 Marunouchi, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo
  • Official/tourism info: B1 has a long bento and delicatessen section with a huge variety of boxed lunches.
  • Store hours checked from Chiyoda tourism page: B1-11F 10:00-20:00. Some shops may differ.

Buy by pairing:

Sake StyleBuy This
clean ginjo / daiginjosushi, sashimi, anago sushi, white fish, dashimaki tamago
food-friendly junmaiseasonal bento, grilled fish, nimono, tofu, simmered vegetables
acidic/nama/moderntempura, karaage, fried oysters, yakitori, cheese, potato salad
yamahai/kimoto/agedmiso-marinated fish, eel, beef bento, pickles, smoked foods

Specific useful counters/categories at Daimaru Tokyo Hoppe Town:

  • 弁当・すし: sushi, bento, anago sushi, seasonal bento.
  • 惣菜: deli sides, fried foods, Chinese/Korean/Japanese deli.
  • 魚介味淋粕漬: sake-friendly fish, especially for richer junmai/yamahai.
  • 漬物: pickles, excellent with sake and rice.
  • 和洋菓子: fruit jelly, wagashi, light sweets for after.

Backup / Add-On: Gransta Tokyo

  • Gransta official basic hours for bento/deli/sweets: weekdays/Sat 08:00-22:00, Sun/holidays 08:00-21:00.
  • Useful if Daimaru is crowded or if you want to stay inside Tokyo Station.
  • Kinokuniya Entree Gransta official page says it carries deli, bread, drinks, alcohol, and otsumami; hours checked 07:00-23:00, Sundays/holidays 07:00-22:00.

Recommended Basket

Elegant Sake Basket

  • Sake: Isojiman, Kuheiji, Zaku, or Hakurakusei.
  • Food: sushi or sashimi set, dashimaki tamago, tofu/simmered vegetables, light pickles.
  • Add: seasonal fruit or small wagashi.

Sake Geek Basket

  • Sake: Kaze no Mori, Senkin, AKABU, Mutsu Hassen, or seasonal nama.
  • Food: tempura, karaage, grilled fish, miso-marinated fish, pickles, cheese or potato salad.
  • Add: sparkling water. Useful between pours.

Low-Energy Premium Basket

  • Sake: one staff-recommended 720ml chilled bottle.
  • Food: one premium bento, one sushi/sashimi pack, one pickle/vegetable side.
  • Why: carrying less and still feels like a serious room dinner.

Practical Notes

  • Buy chilled sake last, then go straight back to Iidabashi.
  • Ask for a cold bag if available: 保冷バッグはありますか？
  • Check whether the room has a fridge before buying too much.
  • If no proper glassware, buy two small cups at convenience store/100-yen shop.
  • Keep one bottle enough. 720ml for two people is already plenty with food.

Final Recommendation

Make 2026-06-02 Tue the room sake night:

Iidabashi -> Tokyo Station
Daimaru Tokyo Hoppe Town or Gransta: buy sushi/sashimi + one fried/grilled side + pickles
Hasegawa Saketen Gransta Tokyo: buy one serious chilled 720ml sake last
Return to Amber Iidabashi
Room sake dinner

This gives the trip one night that feels very Tokyo/Japan without another reservation, line, or late cross-town move.
