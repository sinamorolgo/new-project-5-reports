What This Changes

Pure geek order

Sushi Nanba Hibiya main
or Sushi Akira regular
-> Sushi Ryujiro main
-> Sushi Nishizaki / Sushi Hashimoto
-> Sushi Yuuki
-> Sushi Ao / Sushi Masuda

Trip-realistic order

Sushi Yuuki
-> Sushi Zai
-> Sushi Keita
-> Sushi Hashimoto cancellation
-> Ebisu Endo

The difference matters. A hardcore sushi person would chase Nanba/Akira/Ryujiro/Nishizaki first because those are more interesting as sushi studies. A traveler should still anchor with Yuuki/Zai/Keita because the probability of actually receiving the intended counter experience is higher.

Shop Notes

Sushi Nanba Hibiya

Highest technical evidence. OMAKASE explicitly states the restaurant's idea around shari/neta temperature and even notes that temperature information is given to guests. Tabelog shows 4.51, 2026 Silver, Hyakumeiten, and Gold history from 2020-2024. The main course is the target; Hanare nigiri-only is a different evaluation.

Sushi Akira

Most exciting current-generation pick. Tabelog stresses Sushi Takumi lineage, Wakayama red rice/vinegar identity, and temperature gimmick. OMAKASE confirms regular course and lunch nigiri, but also confirms renovation-related release suspension. Regular course or lunch nigiri is the target; young chef course should be scored separately.

Sushi Ryujiro

Strong if and only if main counter. OMAKASE separates taisho courses and second-counter course, which matters. The Umi lineage and tuna-first identity make it more compelling than a generic high-score pick, but seat type can change the experience.

Sushi Nishizaki

Probably the most "I want to analyze this bite" candidate below the trophy tier. Tabelog emphasizes texture, extremely fine squid work, two shari styles, and unity with rice. Booking route is the weakness, not the sushi logic.

Sushi Hashimoto

Less flashy, more proven. Repeated Silver history, 8-seat counter, OMAKASE route, and strong repeat-review signals make it a high-confidence benchmark. It may not be the most novel, but it is the cleanest "serious Edomae will be good" bet.

Sushi Yuuki

Best travel anchor, not pure geek champion. The scoring is lower than Akira/Nanba because the public signals are less extreme, but the Mizutani lineage, visible OMAKASE route, 9-seat counter, and JPY19,800/JPY33,000 course structure make it unusually strong for this itinerary.

Sushi Zai

Do not over-romanticize it. TableCheck gives a real route and the red-vinegar blended shari / Edomae-plus-modern-ingredients description is promising. But it scores as a practical connoisseur backup, not as a must-chase sushi study.

Decision Rules

  • If Nanba Hibiya main or Akira regular appears: take it unless budget or timing breaks the trip.
  • If Ryujiro appears, confirm taisho/main counter. If second counter, compare against Yuuki before booking.
  • If Nishizaki appears through a verified route, it is a better geek choice than Zai/Keita and possibly more interesting than Yuuki.
  • If only practical slots are visible, Yuuki remains the best first sushi meal.
  • If a second meal is needed after Yuuki, choose Zai for booking safety, Keita for value/lunch learning, Hashimoto for proven high-end.
