Tokyo Sushi Evidence Dossier

Last updated: 2026-05-15 KST

Purpose: tokyo-sushi-omakase-report.md의 결론을 뒷받침하는 근거표. 이 문서는 맛집 소개가 아니라, 후보를 왜 올리고 내렸는지 검증하기 위한 증거 로그다.

For a stricter source-by-source reliability audit, see tokyo-sushi-source-confidence-audit.md. The confidence column below is about whether each candidate is usable for this trip, not a guarantee of live reservation availability or actual taste.

Source Hierarchy

  1. Tabelog Award / Tabelog Sushi Tokyo ranking: broad local signal. Tabelog says its award is based on everyday diner evaluations, with winners selected from highly rated restaurants across about 890,000 restaurants in Japan.
  2. Official booking pages: OMAKASE, TableCheck, Tabelog reservation pages. These decide whether the restaurant is useful for a traveler.
  3. Specialist sushi writing: すしログ is valuable for shari, lineage, temperature, course structure, and chef-specific work.
  4. General blogs / Reddit / travel pages: use only as weak signals, mainly for route discovery or booking anecdotes.

Corrected Ranking Logic

For this trip, not pure prestige

1. Sushi Yuuki
2. Sushi Zai
3. Sushi Keita
4. Sushi Hashimoto cancellation
5. Ebisu Endo

This order is intentionally practical. It gives priority to bookability and price while keeping quality above the "serious sushi" threshold.

For hardcore sushi geeks

1. Sushi Nanba Hibiya main course
2. Sushi Akira regular course
3. Sushi Ryujiro main counter
4. Sushi Nishizaki
5. Sushi Hashimoto
6. Sushi Yuuki
7. Sushi Ao / Sushi Masuda, depending on whether Jiro-lineage orthodoxy or otsumami matters
8. Sushi Keita
9. Sushi Zai

This is the "what would be most interesting at the counter" order, not the safest reservation order. It rewards shari personality, temperature control, lineage transformed into personal style, and main-counter integrity.

If verified cancellation / clean public seat appears

1. Sushi Akira regular course or Sushi Nanba Hibiya main course, if a clean official seat appears
2. Sushi Ryujiro main counter
3. Sushi Nanba Hibiya Hanare, if the goal is nigiri-only at a lower ceiling
4. Sushi Nishizaki
5. Sushi Hashimoto
6. Sushi Ao
7. Sushi Masuda

This order is closer to quality/upside, but it is not the daily booking order.

Important Corrections

  • Sushi Nanba Hibiya should be treated as 2026 Silver with recent Gold history, not as a generic optional pick. The individual Tabelog page is the safer source for the 2026 award label.
  • Sushi Akira was missing from the earlier challenge list. It belongs in the high-upside cancellation watchlist because Tabelog places it above Hashimoto/Ryujiro and OMAKASE confirms an official route.
  • Sushi Nishizaki's quality evidence is strong, but its public booking route should be marked Medium confidence until Pocket Concierge or another official route is verified in-browser.
  • Sushi Zai is not a trophy counter. Its value is practical: high Tabelog score, Hyakumeiten, and a visible TableCheck reservation flow.
